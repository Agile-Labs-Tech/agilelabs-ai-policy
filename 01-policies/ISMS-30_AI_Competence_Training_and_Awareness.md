## 1. Purpose, scope and users

This policy documents the training requirements for all Company personnel — the types of training, who must take them, how often, how competence is assessed, and how completion is evidenced. It covers **both** general cybersecurity training **and** AI-specific training.

**Scope:** all employees, directors and contractors with access to Company systems, information or customer environments.
